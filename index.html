About

Hi, I'm Greg.

I'm a 3D artist based in Lisbon with over 10 years of experience creating images for architects, interior designers, brands and game studios. I started out as an architecture student, fell in love with light and materials, and never looked back.

My work lives between precision and mood: every render begins with accurate geometry, but it is the light, the texture and the small imperfections that make a space feel lived in.

When I'm not rendering, you'll find me sketching on the train, photographing old buildings, or hunting for vintage furniture to model next.
